    Hands-on by default. I prefer work that leaves the simulator and runs on a board, a bench or a robot.

    I'm a third-year electrical engineering student at ENIT (École Nationale d'Ingénieurs de Tunis), in the Automation & Industrial Computing option: control systems, robotics and industrial IoT.

    I'm equally at home designing the system around the controller: routing a board, writing VHDL for an FPGA, scheduling tasks on an RTOS, and building the software and dashboards on top. That range lets me take a machine from a block diagram to a working prototype.

    At ACTIA Engineering Services I built a battery digital twin fusing an Extended Kalman Filter with XGBoost to estimate state of charge, deployed and validated on ARM64.
